\section{Results}

\subsection{Study sample and data throughput}
We restrict attention to residential customers labeled \emph{Particuliers} in the distributor metadata and retain only those smart-meter histories whose cumulative consumption satisfies $\sum \mathrm{CONSO} \leq 100$~MWh over the available record (streaming scan over parquet archives). We then compute PV-oriented indicators merged with regional irradiance and remove a small set of physically inconsistent indicator rows (anomaly filter) before capacity estimation. After this sequence, the cohort contains $N_{\mathrm{meta}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ Particuliers identifiers in metadata, $N_{\mathrm{scan}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ distinct Particuliers with meter data observed in the scan, $N_{\mathrm{cap}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ customers remaining after the consumption cap, $N_{\mathrm{ind}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ with computed indicators, and $N_{\mathrm{clean}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ after the anomaly filter (\texttt{pv\_indicators\_clean}). Portfolio capacity totals below sum only customers with strictly positive hybrid capacity estimates; that subset has size $N_{+} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ (\texttt{portfolio\_agg["n\_customers"]}).

\subsection{PV-related prevalence (screening only)}
Among customers in \texttt{pv\_indicators\_clean}, the binary indicator \texttt{has\_pv} flags $p_{\mathrm{pv}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ of sites (or $K_{\mathrm{pv}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ of $N_{\mathrm{clean}}$) as PV-like under the internal correlation-based rule implemented in \texttt{classify\_pv\_customers}. The auxiliary score \texttt{has\_pv\_prob} is a heuristic rescaling of the production--irradiance association and should not be read as a calibrated probability. In the absence of external PV labels or nameplate registers, these quantities serve only as an \emph{internal screening signal} for downstream modelling, not as a validated detection benchmark.

\subsection{Portfolio installed capacity and uncertainty brackets}
Aggregating hybrid point estimates \texttt{pv\_capacity\_kwp} for customers with capacity $> 0$ yields a portfolio total of $C_{\mathrm{tot}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$~kWp (\texttt{portfolio\_agg["total\_hybrid\_kwp"]}). Because estimation errors across customers share regional drivers yet are not perfectly aligned, we report two conservative \emph{brackets} (not claims of frequentist coverage against ground truth): a \textbf{conservative} interval $[C^{-}_{\mathrm{cons}}, C^{+}_{\mathrm{cons}}] = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$~kWp formed by summing per-customer bootstrap interval endpoints (\texttt{portfolio\_agg["ci\_conservative"]}), and an \textbf{independence} interval $[C^{-}_{\mathrm{ind}}, C^{+}_{\mathrm{ind}}] = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$~kWp from a square-root sum of per-customer variances (\texttt{portfolio\_agg["ci\_independence"]}). The plausible portfolio uncertainty lies between these dependence assumptions; we do not assert which bound is closer to a hypothetical true total without independent capacity observations.

\subsection{Supporting diagnostic: floor versus regression agreement}
As a single aggregate diagnostic complementary to the hybrid total, the ratio of summed floor-type capacities to summed regression-only capacities is $R_{\mathrm{f2r}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ (\texttt{portfolio\_agg["portfolio\_floor\_to\_reg\_ratio"]}). Values near unity indicate broad agreement between the two construction paths; systematic departures suggest heterogeneous export behaviour, data sparsity, or structural mismatch worth inspecting in case studies (supplementary material), without implying correctness against external registers.

\subsection{Internal plausibility screening}
We summarise implied specific yields and flag customers outside a literature-informed band (defaults $800$--$1200$~kWh/kWp/yr in \texttt{evaluate\_portfolio}) using \texttt{yield\_df["yield\_flag"]}. Counts are: plausible $= \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}$, low $= \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}$, high $= \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}$, no data $= \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ (from \texttt{evaluation["yield\_stats"]["flag\_counts"]} or \texttt{yield\_df["yield\_flag"].value\_counts()}). These flags are \emph{sanity screens} on internal consistency between estimated capacity, metered export, and modelled self-consumption; they do not constitute validation against measured nameplate or independent generation surveys. Optional segment-level fences populate \texttt{evaluation["capacity\_flag\_counts"]} if reported.

\subsection{Full-year forecast artefact (internal consistency)}
For calendar year $Y = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ (\texttt{FORECAST\_YEAR}), we wrote a full-year, 15-minute PV generation forecast table to \texttt{[FILL: path]} (\texttt{FORECAST\_OUTPUT\_PATH}). A lightweight portfolio check (\texttt{validate\_portfolio\_forecast}) sums forecast energy $E_{\mathrm{fc}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$~kWh across all rows and compares it to the summed estimated capacities $C_{\mathrm{sum}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$~kWp from \texttt{prob\_summary}, giving an implied portfolio specific yield $E_{\mathrm{fc}} / C_{\mathrm{sum}} = \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$~kWh/kWp (\texttt{total\_forecast\_kwh}, \texttt{total\_capacity\_kwp}, \texttt{portfolio\_specific\_yield\_kwh\_kwp}). The boolean outcome \texttt{ok} $= \text{\texttt{[FILL]}}}$ flags gross inconsistency against a generous upper yield threshold; this is an internal coherence check and is secondary to the capacity totals above.
